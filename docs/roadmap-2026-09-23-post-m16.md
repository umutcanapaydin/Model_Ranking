# Roadmap snapshot — after M16 (2026-09-23)

A snapshot, never edited after it is written (seed I.1).

**Where the product is.** One application on the owner's Mac: an engine that refreshes itself once
a night, fetches every board it reads, carries a failed source for 30 days, and registers new models
from the boards and price feeds under the owner's "the list wins" rule; an iOS app that routes a
question to one of fourteen surfaces on the device and shows three answers with their evidence.
Nothing is deployed beyond the owner's machine.

**What is next.** M17 (`docs/plans/m17-plan.md`): more boards (Arena's category slices, Epoch's own
boards), and a question read on the device by Apple Intelligence that reaches a combination of
boards, so the product can answer with lists no leaderboard publishes.

**After M17, not yet planned.** A first deploy beyond the owner's machine, which needs the data
licences settled (W-129) and a stranger's first use of the app, which is the only instrument that
says what people ask.
