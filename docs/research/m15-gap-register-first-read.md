# M15 — the gap register's first read

**Owed by `docs/plans/m15-plan.md` §2 W1 ("whatever it says ... if it is empty, that is a finding
too") and §4, and not done in W1. Read at W4 on 2026-09-22 by the lead agent.**

## What was read, and how

The gap register lives only on the device (M14-W3, D-126): `Application Support/GapRegister/
gap-register.json` in the app's container, backup-excluded. There is no phone install; the only
containers are the owner's simulators. One file exists across all of them, on the iPhone 17 Pro
simulator (`0EC5C12C-…`), 86 bytes, last written 2026-09-22 02:33 local time.

It was read on the owner's Mac and **nothing typed was copied out of it**: this repository is
public, and D-126 says the reader's words never leave the device. This record states counts and a
theme, never the text.

## What that means

- **The instrument works end to end.** An image-editing question declined, and the decline was kept
  with its count, in its own folder. That is REQ-ASK-003 and the M14-W3 register behaving as
  designed. It also agrees with D-147: making or changing an image is one of the decline groups,
  and no board here ranks it.
- **It is not evidence of demand.** The one entry is the owner's own test, and no stranger has used
  the app (handover §1). The register cannot say what readers ask until readers exist.
- **So the direction the owner set (cover the questions people actually ask) cannot be steered by
  this instrument yet.** For M16, that makes public corpora of real prompts the only available
  instrument, as the handover's §7 already anticipated. The register becomes useful after the first
  TestFlight or equivalent, and not before.

## What this record does not claim

It does not claim the register is correct under load, across app updates, or on a real device;
none of those has happened.
