THE LESSON M7 PAID FOR
**A control that runs on every request can still be the defect, and no reachability tool will say
so. The question is not "does this execute" but "should this exist".** `serving_snapshot` ran on
every request for a whole milestone — cited, tested, measured three times, given a five-constant
budget. Deleting the WRITE that forced it removed the control, the budget, the tests and the entire
class of finding at once.

Corollary, paid twice: **deleting a test needs the same care as writing one.** W3 removed three
tests with the budget they guarded; one also guarded a live control, and two mutants walked through.
