THE LESSON M12 PAID FOR

regret. The 28-row inventory that drove this milestone came from a seat told to read the product as

a CHAIR rather than a check.

when text MOVES teaches people to leave text where it is — which is how a user-facing string ends up
explaining App Transport Security to a CFO.
